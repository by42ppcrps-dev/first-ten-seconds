Privacy

  • Your video never leaves your computer. It is opened in your browser as a local file.
  • For each shot checked, one 1024 by 288 JPEG (your screenshot next to one frame) is sent to the server and passed to Cloudflare Workers AI. This site does not store or log the picture.
  • So the same picture is never paid for twice, the server keeps the model's short answer (how much of the frame is software, and whether there is a headline or a photo) under a SHA-256 fingerprint of the picture. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into the picture.
  • To enforce daily limits, the server keeps a count of looks per visitor under a salted hash of your IP address and the date. It is deleted within about 36 hours. No raw IP addresses, file names or images are kept.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests. Fonts (Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License) are served from this site.
