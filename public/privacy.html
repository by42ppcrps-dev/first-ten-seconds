Privacy

  • Your video never leaves your computer. It is opened in your browser as a local file.
  • For each shot checked, one 1024 by 288 JPEG (your screenshot next to one frame) is sent to the server, passed to Cloudflare Workers AI, and not stored or logged.
  • To enforce daily limits, the server keeps a count of looks per visitor under a salted hash of your IP address and the date. It is deleted within about 36 hours. No raw IP addresses, file names or images are kept.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests. Fonts (Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License) are served from this site.
